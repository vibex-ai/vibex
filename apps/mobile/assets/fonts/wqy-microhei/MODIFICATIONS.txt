Modification (both faces of the collection, "WenQuanYi Micro Hei" and
"WenQuanYi Micro Hei Mono"):

  The upstream `hmtx` table declares a horizontal advance of 256 units
  (1/8 em) for 11,390 glyphs whose outlines are a full em wide: 11,170 of
  the Hangul syllables (U+AC00..U+D7A3; two already carried the right
  advance), the two covered CJK extension A ideographs, and 218 CJK
  compatibility ideographs (U+F900..U+FA2D). HarfBuzz honours the advance,
  so Korean text shaped with this face draws each syllable on top of the
  next and the whole line collapses into a smear.

  Those 11,390 entries were rewritten to 2048 units (1 em, the advance the
  same face already gives every Han ideograph and kana). No outline, cmap,
  name or vertical metric was touched. The rewrite was made in place, so
  table offsets and the file length are unchanged; only `hmtx` bytes, the
  per-face table checksums in the collection directory and each face's
  `head.checkSumAdjustment` differ from upstream.

  The 24 "small form" punctuation glyphs (U+FE50..U+FE6B) keep their
  intentional 1024-unit advance.
